# Fibroids (medically managed)

<!-- concept_ids: (completed by data team) -->
<!-- author: derived from GIRFT Clinically-led Gynaecology Outpatient Guide (July 2023) | reviewed_by: <lead> | last_updated: 2026-09 -->

## Scope

Fibroids managed medically (not surgically). Surgically managed fibroids follow the
surgically-treated-conditions pathway in `general-principles.md`.

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Criterion: PIFU suitable following successful treatment.
  Supported when: letter confirms symptoms and fibroid size are stable on the current medical
  management.

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Fibroid enlargement or new/worsening symptoms warranting surgical or interventional referral
  (e.g. myomectomy, uterine artery embolisation).
- Treatment being initiated or titrated, with response not yet confirmed.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Symptoms resolved after treatment, with no ongoing specialist need.

## Borderline / uncertain (resolve to RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP, lower confidence)

- No recent imaging to confirm fibroid size is stable.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Fibroid size/number on imaging | Confirms stability vs enlargement |
| Symptom severity | Confirms symptom control |
| Treatment given | Distinguishes stable PIFU from active titration |

## Surveillance / review interval

PIFU 6 months; triggers for return appointment are persisting symptoms or fibroid enlargement;
remote appointment type (with imaging arranged as needed to assess enlargement).

# Heavy or irregular menstrual bleeding

<!-- concept_ids: (completed by data team) -->
<!-- author: derived from GIRFT Clinically-led Gynaecology Outpatient Guide (July 2023) | reviewed_by: <lead> | last_updated: 2026-09 -->

## Scope

Heavy menstrual bleeding (HMB) and irregular menstrual bleeding managed medically or surgically.
Excludes post-menopausal bleeding and post-coital bleeding, which require face-to-face assessment
to exclude sinister pathology (see `general-principles.md`).

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Criterion: PIFU suitable following successful treatment.
  Supported when: letter confirms bleeding pattern is acceptable to the patient on the current
  medical or surgical management.

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Treatment being initiated or titrated, with response not yet confirmed.
- Investigations pending (e.g. pipelle biopsy, hysteroscopy, ultrasound).
- Endometrial biopsy required as part of ongoing assessment — per NICE guidance this requires a
  face-to-face, not remote, appointment.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Symptoms resolved after treatment, with no ongoing specialist need.

## Borderline / uncertain (resolve to RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP, lower confidence)

- Bleeding pattern described as "improved" without confirmation it is acceptable to the patient.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Bleeding pattern and impact on patient | Confirms symptom control |
| Treatment given (medical/surgical) | Determines stability |
| Biopsy/histology result, if performed | Excludes need for face-to-face review |

## Surveillance / review interval

PIFU 6 months; trigger for return appointment is persisting symptoms; remote appointment type,
except where an endometrial biopsy is required (NICE), which needs a face-to-face appointment.

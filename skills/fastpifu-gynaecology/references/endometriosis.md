# Endometriosis

## Scope

Endometriosis managed medically or surgically in secondary-care gynaecology, covering both
mild/moderate and severe presentations. Severity changes the PIFU timescale but not the
underlying disposition logic.

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Criterion: Mild/moderate endometriosis following successful treatment.
  Supported when: letter confirms treatment completed/response reviewed and pain is controlled on
  current management. PIFU for up to 6 months, patient re-contacts on exacerbation of pain.
- Criterion: Severe endometriosis following successful treatment.
  Supported when: as above but disease is classified as severe (e.g. deep infiltrating disease,
  bowel/bladder involvement, prior complex surgery). PIFU for up to 12 months, patient re-contacts
  on exacerbation of pain.

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Hormonal or medical therapy newly started or being titrated, with response not yet confirmed.
- Persisting or worsening pain despite treatment, or pending further imaging/laparoscopy.
- Severe endometriosis awaiting or under active surgical management (e.g. complex laparoscopic
  excision with bowel/bladder/ureteric involvement) — MDT-level care.
- Fertility concerns being actively investigated or managed alongside endometriosis.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Symptoms fully resolved after treatment, with no ongoing specialist role and a documented route
  back to the service if pain recurs.

## Borderline / uncertain (resolve to RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP, lower confidence)

- Treatment response described as "some improvement" without a clear statement that pain is
  controlled.
- Severity (mild/moderate vs severe) not explicitly classified in the letter.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic confirmation (imaging/laparoscopy findings) | Confirms diagnosis and severity |
| Severity classification (mild/moderate vs severe) | Sets PIFU timescale (6 vs 12 months) |
| Current treatment and documented response | Distinguishes PIFU-suitable from still-titrating |

## Surveillance / review interval

PIFU 6 months (mild/moderate) or 12 months (severe); trigger for return appointment is
exacerbation of pain; remote appointment type.

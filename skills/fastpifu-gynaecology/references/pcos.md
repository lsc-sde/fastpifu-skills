# Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)

## Scope

PCOS as a chronic, stable condition managed long-term in secondary or shared care, covering
menstrual, metabolic and dermatological features.

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Criterion: Chronic, stable PCOS.
  Supported when: letter confirms diagnosis established (per Rotterdam or equivalent criteria) and
  patient stable on the agreed management plan (lifestyle, menstrual regulation, metabolic
  monitoring as indicated).

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Diagnosis not yet confirmed or investigations pending.
- New or escalating symptoms requiring specialist input (e.g. fertility treatment planning,
  metabolic complications, endometrial protection concerns).

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Stable, long-term condition manageable entirely in primary care once a plan is established, with
  a documented route back to the service if symptoms change.

## Borderline / uncertain (resolve to RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP, lower confidence)

- Diagnosis stated but management plan not documented.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Diagnostic criteria met | Confirms PCOS diagnosis |
| Current symptom control | Determines stability |
| Metabolic monitoring status (if applicable) | Identifies need for ongoing specialist input |

## Surveillance / review interval

PIFU 6 months; trigger for return appointment is persisting symptoms; remote appointment type.

# Urinary incontinence (uncomplicated)

<!-- concept_ids: (completed by data team) -->
<!-- author: derived from GIRFT Clinically-led Gynaecology Outpatient Guide (July 2023) | reviewed_by: <lead> | last_updated: 2026-09 -->

## Scope

Uncomplicated urinary incontinence managed conservatively via pelvic floor physiotherapy or a
continence nurse specialist. Distinct from procedural/surgical management of incontinence (see
`bulking-agents.md`).

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Not a routine PIFU pathway — discharge is the default outcome following successful conservative
  management (see DISCHARGE).

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Symptoms not responding to conservative management.
- Being considered for surgical or medical escalation.
- Complex or mixed incontinence requiring urodynamics or MDT input.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Following successful conservative management by a physiotherapist or continence nurse
  specialist — no PIFU, direct discharge with a documented route back if symptoms recur.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Type of incontinence (stress/urge/mixed) | Confirms uncomplicated presentation |
| Conservative management undertaken and response | Confirms successful management for discharge |

## Surveillance / review interval

No PIFU interval — direct discharge following successful conservative management; re-refer if
symptoms recur.

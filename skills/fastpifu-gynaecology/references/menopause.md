# Menopause

## Scope

Menopause and perimenopause managed in secondary care. Excludes post-menopausal bleeding, which is
a new presentation requiring face-to-face assessment to exclude sinister pathology (see
`general-principles.md`), never a PIFU trigger.

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Criterion: PIFU suitable following consultation including treatment/guidance.
  Supported when: letter confirms the patient has received treatment/guidance (e.g. HRT initiation
  or lifestyle advice) and is established and stable on the current management.

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Treatment being initiated or titrated, with response not yet confirmed.
- Complex case (e.g. premature ovarian insufficiency, contraindications to HRT, unresolved
  treatment side effects) requiring ongoing specialist review.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Stable on management with no ongoing specialist need — can be managed by the GP, with a
  documented route back to the service if symptoms change.

## Borderline / uncertain (resolve to RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP, lower confidence)

- New bleeding reported while on HRT — this needs assessment, not routine PIFU continuation.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Menopause status/type (natural, premature ovarian insufficiency, surgical) | Determines complexity |
| Treatment given and response | Confirms stability |
| Any bleeding on HRT | Requires assessment before PIFU |

## Surveillance / review interval

PIFU 6 months; trigger for return appointment is persisting symptoms; remote appointment type.

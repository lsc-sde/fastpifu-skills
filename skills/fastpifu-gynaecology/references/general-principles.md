## Scope

Cross-cutting guidance for gynaecology outpatient follow-up: remote-consultation suitability,
discharge pathways for surgically and medically treated conditions, and the guide's "top tips".
Read alongside the condition-specific files.

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Surgically treated conditions (minor, complication-free): PIFU after successful treatment for
  3–6 months then discharge if no clinical concerns.
- Surgically treated conditions (complex, complication-free): PIFU after successful treatment for
  6–12 months then discharge if no clinical concerns.
- Medically treated conditions in general: PIFU after successful treatment for 6–12 months then
  discharge if no clinical concerns.

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Vulval conditions — physical examination is needed; not appropriate for remote review or PIFU.
- Post-menopausal bleeding — needs assessment to rule out concerning features/sinister pathology;
  not a remote or PIFU disposition.
- Abnormal vaginal bleeding — needs assessment to rule out concerning features/sinister pathology;
  not a remote or PIFU disposition.
- Post-coital bleeding — requires physical examination; not a remote or PIFU disposition.
- Any 2-week-wait cancer referral — excluded from the remote-first approach.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Conservative treatment recommended and diagnostics do not indicate a need for treatment —
  discharge following negative investigations and review, no PIFU.
- Urinary incontinence (uncomplicated) — following successful conservative management by
  physiotherapy or a continence nurse specialist (see `urinary-incontinence.md`).
- Pelvic floor prolapse (uncomplicated) — no PIFU, direct discharge (see
  `pelvic-organ-prolapse.md`).

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Consultation modality planned (remote vs face-to-face) | Some conditions cannot be safely assessed remotely |
| Whether the condition is on the remote-caution list (vulval, PMB, AVB, PCB) | Excludes remote/PIFU disposition |
| Treatment type and complexity (medical/surgical, minor/complex) | Sets the applicable PIFU timescale |

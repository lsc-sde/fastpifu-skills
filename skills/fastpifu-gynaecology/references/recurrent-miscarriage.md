# Recurrent miscarriage

<!-- concept_ids: (completed by data team) -->
<!-- author: derived from GIRFT Clinically-led Gynaecology Outpatient Guide (July 2023) | reviewed_by: <lead> | last_updated: 2026-09 -->

## Scope

Recurrent miscarriage (typically ≥3 consecutive pregnancy losses) investigated and managed via a
recurrent miscarriage clinic.

## PIFU — suitable for patient-initiated follow-up

- Criterion: PIFU suitable following successful treatment.
  Supported when: letter confirms investigations are complete and a management plan is in place
  for any future pregnancy.

## RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP — timed specialist follow-up still needed

- Investigations pending (e.g. thrombophilia screen, parental karyotype, anatomical/endocrine
  work-up).
- Treatment being initiated or titrated.
- Active pregnancy requiring early pregnancy unit (EPU) surveillance.

## DISCHARGE — no ongoing specialist follow-up needed

- Investigations complete (cause found and treated, or no cause found) with a documented plan, and
  no ongoing specialist follow-up need outside of a future pregnancy.

## Borderline / uncertain (resolve to RETAIN_FOLLOW_UP, lower confidence)

- Investigations described as "complete" without a documented management plan for a future
  pregnancy.

## Data that must be present to assess

| Required data point | Why it matters |
|---|---|
| Investigation results (thrombophilia, karyotype, anatomical, endocrine) | Confirms work-up complete |
| Management plan for future pregnancy | Required before PIFU/discharge |
| Current pregnancy status | Active pregnancy needs EPU surveillance, not PIFU |

## Surveillance / review interval

PIFU 6 months; trigger for return appointment is persisting symptoms; remote appointment type.
Direct access to the EPU should be arranged for any new pregnancy.
